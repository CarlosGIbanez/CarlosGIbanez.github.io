## Books

### Machine Learning & AI

- [Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach](https://www.amazon.com/Artificial-Intelligence-A-Modern-Approach/dp/0134610997) by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig. The standard AI textbook.
- [The Elements of Statistical Learning](https://www.amazon.com/Elements-Statistical-Learning-Prediction-Statistics/dp/0387848576) by Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani and Jerome Friedman. A rigorous tour of supervised and unsupervised learning methods from a statistical point of view.
- [Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction](https://www.amazon.com/Reinforcement-Learning-Introduction-Adaptive-Computation/dp/0262039249) by Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto. The foundational text on RL.
- [Superintelligence](https://www.amazon.com/Superintelligence-Dangers-Strategies-Nick-Bostrom/dp/0198739834) by Nick Bostrom. What might happen if machines surpass human general intelligence, and why controlling them is hard.

### Software Engineering

- [A Philosophy of Software Design](https://www.amazon.com/Philosophy-Software-Design-2nd/dp/173210221X) by John Ousterhout. Managing complexity through deep modules, good abstractions and early warning signs of bad design.

### Economics & Causal Inference

- [Why Nations Fail](https://www.amazon.com/Why-Nations-Fail-Origins-Prosperity/dp/0307719227) by Daron Acemoglu and James A. Robinson. Inclusive versus extractive political and economic institutions explain why some countries prosper and others don't.
- [The Dictator's Handbook](https://www.amazon.com/Dictators-Handbook-Behavior-Almost-Politics/dp/1541701364) by Bruce Bueno de Mesquita and Alastair Smith. Leaders of every regime act to keep the support of the coalition that keeps them in power.
- [Principles of Economics](https://www.amazon.com/Principles-Economics-9th-Gregory-Mnkiw/dp/0357541596) by N. Gregory Mankiw. The classic introductory textbook, a clear foundation in micro- and macroeconomics.
- [Causal Inference: The Mixtape](https://mixtape.scunning.com/) by Scott Cunningham. A practical guide to causal methods (matching, IV, regression discontinuity, diff-in-diff, synthetic control).
- [Causal Inference in Statistics: A Primer](https://www.amazon.com/Causal-Inference-Statistics-Judea-Pearl/dp/1119186846) by Judea Pearl, Madelyn Glymour and Nicholas P. Jewell. A short introduction to causal graphs, interventions and counterfactuals.
- [Philosophy of Economics](https://www.amazon.com/Philosophy-Economics-Palgrave-Today/dp/0230302971) by Don Ross. A concise look at what economics explains, how it relates to other sciences, and its role in public policy.

### Rationality & Epistemology

- [The Beginning of Infinity](https://www.amazon.com/Beginning-Infinity-Explanations-Transform-World/dp/0143121359) by David Deutsch. Good explanations drive all human progress, and this progress can be unbounded.
- [Superforecasting](https://www.amazon.com/Superforecasting-Science-Prediction-Philip-Tetlock/dp/0804136718) by Philip E. Tetlock and Dan Gardner. Lessons from the Good Judgment Project on what makes some people much better at predicting the future.
- [Focusing](https://www.amazon.com/Focusing-Eugene-T-Gendlin/dp/0553278339) by Eugene T. Gendlin. A six-step method for noticing the bodily "felt sense" of a problem and letting it shift.
- [The Scout Mindset](https://www.amazon.com/Scout-Mindset-Perils-Defensive-Thinking/dp/0735217556) by Julia Galef. Why wanting to see things as they are, rather than defending what you already believe, leads to better judgment.
- [Rationality: From AI to Zombies](https://www.readthesequences.com/) by Eliezer Yudkowsky. The LessWrong Sequences: essays on cognitive biases, evidence and changing your mind.

### Personal Effectiveness

- [The Book of Five Rings](https://www.amazon.com/Book-Five-Rings-Miyamoto-Musashi/dp/1590309847) by Miyamoto Musashi. A 17th-century swordsman's treatise on strategy, discipline and mastery.
- [Tiny Experiments](https://www.amazon.com/Tiny-Experiments-Freely-Goal-Obsessed-World/dp/1800819153) by Anne-Laure Le Cunff. Proposes replacing rigid goals with small, curiosity-driven experiments on how you live and work.
- [Designing the Mind](https://www.amazon.com/Designing-Mind-Principles-Psychitecture/dp/B08SGWNLV9) by Ryan A. Bush. Treats the mind as software that can be deliberately redesigned, drawing on Stoicism, Taoism and psychology.

### Ethics & Political Philosophy

- [The Road to Serfdom](https://www.amazon.com/Road-Serfdom-Classic-Inherent-Planning/dp/0226320782) by Friedrich A. Hayek. A warning that central economic planning erodes individual liberty.
- [A Theory of Justice](https://www.amazon.com/Theory-Justice-Revised-Belknap-ebook/dp/B09543474Q) by John Rawls. Derives principles of justice from the "original position", choosing a society's rules behind a veil of ignorance.
- [Animal Liberation Now](https://www.amazon.com/Animal-Liberation-Now-Definitive-Classic/dp/0063226707) by Peter Singer. The case against speciesism and for taking animal suffering seriously.
- [Doing Good Better](https://www.amazon.com/Doing-Good-Better-Effective-Altruism/dp/1592409660) by William MacAskill. An introduction to effective altruism: using evidence and careful reasoning to do the most good.
- [The Open Society and Its Enemies](https://www.amazon.com/Open-Society-Enemies-Princeton-Classics/dp/0691210845) by Karl Popper. A defense of liberal democracy and critical thinking against the totalitarian ideas.

### Business & Finance

- [The Intelligent Investor](https://www.amazon.com/Intelligent-Investor-Definitive-Investing-Essentials/dp/0060555661) by Benjamin Graham. The classic on value investing.
- [The Lean Startup](https://www.amazon.com/Lean-Startup-Entrepreneurs-Continuous-Innovation/dp/0307887898) by Eric Ries. Build products through fast build–measure–learn experiments rather than long plans.
