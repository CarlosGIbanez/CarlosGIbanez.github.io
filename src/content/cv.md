## Research & Projects

### Monetary Policy Recommendation for Banco de México

> 2026 | Reto Banxico — team submission, selected among the top 11

Documentary and data-analysis research producing a monetary policy recommendation, combining macroeconomic evidence with an institutional analysis of Banco de México's policy framework drawn from New Institutional Economics (central bank independence, credible commitment, and the conditions under which policy announcements are believed).

My contribution: data-analysis research, institutional analysis of Banco de Mexico's monetary policy from the lens of the NIE, elaboration of the monetary policy recommendation.

We recommended pausing the rate-cutting cycle until inflation expectations showed clear convergence toward the 3% target, arguing that premature easing would trade a short-term growth gain against the central bank's most valuable asset: its credibility.

### Inflation Expectations and Monetary Policy Transmission in Mexico

> 2026 | Coursework, Macroeconomics | Team project (4 members)

Vector autoregression (VAR) model of Mexican inflation, inflation expectations, and the monetary policy rate.

My contribution: exploratory analysis in Python, stationarity and unit-roots detection, VAR models, rolling-window evaluation design.

Estimated a VAR with impulse-response and forecast-error variance decomposition analysis
Modeled conditional heteroskedasticity in the residuals with DCC-GARCH to capture time-varying comovement across series
Evaluated out-of-sample forecast performance using rolling-window estimation

Tools: R, Python, LaTeX

[Check the repo here](https://github.com/CarlosGIbanez/Inflation-Expectations-Forecasting)
