> A mindset answers the question: how should I think about X?

Mindsets are about shaping the default mind state in a given situation. Is kind of an habit where the reaction itself is a mental state.

## The power of mindsets

Mindsets are so powerful because, by changing how you think, they allow you to change your behavior for the better in a wide range of situations.

Problems are mostly a matter of ignorance or believing (thinking) the wrong thing, so once you correct your beliefs to better reflect reality, your behavior changes dramatically.

E.g. "My skills are fixed, I can only be so good" (Fixed mindset). This is false; correcting your belief will bring you to "I can improve my skills, the limit to my output is determined only by my strategic effort" (Growth mindset).

## Some good mindsets

- In general:
  - **[Growth mindset](https://ctl.stanford.edu/students/growth-mindset)**: Intelligence, skills and talents are not fixed, they can be trained and improved with strategic effort
  - **Being proactive**: Don't wait for things, make them happen
- On conversations: **Seek excitement**, both from the other person's perspective and from your own
- On productivity: **Make strategic/intelligent effort**. Don't push the "try-harder button", instead find the most efficient way to do what you want
- On learning:
  - **Mental model building**: Learning is the process of building and storing mental models in your head
  - **Notice knowledge gaps**: Errors in the learning process are expected, and the default. Your job is to find them and correct them

## Building mindsets

Building a mindset is easy. Just remember to think in a certain way in a certain situation, and soon you'll start doing it unconsciously; you made an habit! It's so simple that it feels almost stupid. Give it a try!
